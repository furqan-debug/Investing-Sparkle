The ground, the type, the rhythm.

Everything downstream is assembled from these values. They are deliberately narrow: a single green ground, a single gold action, two text families and one mono for numbers. Restraint is what makes the brand feel authored.

Rhythm, not noise. Sections breathe at 72 to 128 px. Cards use 24 to 28 px of internal padding. Radii stay under 28 px except for pills; oversized rounded corners are what make an interface look like a template.

Glass and elevation

One recipe / two shadows

Surface / glass

A card should feel lifted, not frosted.

Three to five percent white, a hairline border, a single inner highlight. Blur does the rest. 

/* surface */
background: linear-gradient(
  180deg,
  rgba(255,255,255,.055),
  rgba(255,255,255,.022)
);
backdrop-filter:
  blur(22px) saturate(140%);
border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.075);
border-radius: 20px;

/* depth */
box-shadow:
  0 40px 90px -40px rgba(0,0,0,.9),
  inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.07);

No white panels. Glass on dark reads as a material only when the fill stays under 6% white. Above that it turns grey, the text contrast collapses, and the premium feel disappears.

Three rules that keep it honest

Apply to every page

RULE 01

One gold action per view.

The eye should know where to go without reading. Secondary actions are glass or text, never another gold button.

RULE 02

Structure with hairlines, not boxes.

A 1 px line at 7.5% white separates without adding weight. Nested cards are banned; one surface level is enough.

RULE 03

Numbers are always mono.

Prices, percentages, dates and returns use tabular figures. This is a financial interface, not a magazine.

02

Parts, not pages.

Three components carry most of the product: the action, the glass card and the input. Each is specified once and reused everywhere. Hover them.

04

Three calls before this becomes the site.

The tokens are ready. What I need from you is a decision on three directions, not a line-by-line review. Everything else can be tuned without changing the system.

Decision / v1.0

The direction is deliberately quieter than the current site.

Darker ground, tighter type, one accent, fewer decorative elements. That is the premium shift. If any of these three feels wrong, say so now, because every page will inherit them.

01 Type direction

Instrument Sans for headings and Inter for reading, or keep DM Serif Display as the heading face? The serif still appears once per page as an accent.

02 Gold temperature

#EBCE86 is softer and more expensive than the current #DDAF33. It also passes contrast at 13.2:1. Confirm the warmer, paler gold.

03 Density

This is tighter than the live site: 72 to 128 px sections, 16 px body. If you want more air, we raise the rhythm, not the type.

Nothing on the live site has changed. This page is additive: a single file at /design-system.html, importing nothing and imported by nothing. Once the tokens are approved, the next step is to replace app/globals.css and rebuild the header and homepage hero first, so you can judge the system in context before the remaining pages follow.
